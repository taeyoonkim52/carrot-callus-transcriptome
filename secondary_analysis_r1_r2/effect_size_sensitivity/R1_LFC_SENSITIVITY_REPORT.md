**Sensitivity A.** A single PyDESeq2 coefficient's lfc_shrink is not a valid shrinker of a nonlinear four-genotype range. The first normal approximation used the saved HMH covariance exactly as predeclared, but failed to moderate the largest poorly identified contrast. That initial result is retained in R1_LFC_INITIAL_HMH_DIAGNOSTIC.tsv and R1_LFC_GENE_DETAILS.tsv and must not be presented as successful tail control. The issue and method change are disclosed in R1_SHRINKAGE_METHOD_ADDENDUM.md, written before calculating the alternative.

The reported A rows instead use the saturated eight-cell model's expected-Fisher variances: Var(log cell mean)=1/sum(mu/(1+alpha*mu)); Var(genotype response)=sum of its two cell variances/log(2)^2. With disjoint cells these four contrasts are independent conditional on saved dispersions and means. Posterior mean b*tau²/(tau²+variance), with unchanged tau=1 and 2 priors. This is an approximate normal-likelihood sensitivity, not apeglm, not a refit, and not a fully Bayesian NB analysis. Near-boundary normality and prior dependence remain limitations. Maximum ranges become 10.5194 and 14.5616; LARGE remains 68.97% and 70.19%.

**Sensitivity B.** CPM>=1 and companion CPM>=5 in >=2/3 dishes in EACH of eight genotype/time cells; saved CPM and original unshrunk effects only. Retained n=10,862/8,353; LARGE=59.12%/48.84%; practical responses remain 10,295/7,786, or 41.24%/31.19% of the original universe. This deliberately excludes genuine on/off responses and is not a replacement universe. No support threshold was tuned to outcomes.

**Numerical warning.** Two of 24,961 genes meet information condition>10^12 or minimum cell information<10^-6. One is in the original significant interaction set: 108213928 (range32.8185, original interaction FDR0, condition2.26×10^20, minimum cell information1.30×10^-20). Gene135152991 was already invalid/non-significant. Neither is core. HMH regularization can give unreliable precision near this boundary; the exact FDR and maximum of 108213928 should not be treated as reliable evidence. This is a targeted warning, not a demonstration that all interaction p-values are invalid. No primary value was silently corrected. A future explicitly authorized numerical inference audit is required before validating that exact gene-level claim.

**Conclusion.** Broad practical heterogeneity persists under all four checks (at least31.19% of the original universe). The claim that LARGE always forms a majority is not robust to the strongest support filter. Classification PARTIALLY_ROBUST follows the declared rule: practical>=10% persists, but LARGE>=50% does not hold in every check. The central broad-response architecture remains supported, while its effect-size proportions and extreme-tail precision need qualification. No sensitivity was treated as independent validation.
