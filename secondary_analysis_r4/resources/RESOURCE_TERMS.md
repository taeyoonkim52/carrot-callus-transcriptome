# Resource terms and scope

Pinned NCBI RefSeq DH1 v3.0 and Arabidopsis TAIR10.1 protein/feature snapshots are third-party reference material. Their accession, URLs, provider checksums and SHA256 are in RESOURCE_MANIFEST.json. Their terms are not replaced by the project MIT license. Baseline Ensembl/BioMart/GO snapshots and their original terms remain in secondary_analysis_r1_r2/resources/. BLAST+ source/binary provenance is recorded but executables/caches are not included. Do not assert ownership or apply blanket licensing to source sequencing or external annotations.
